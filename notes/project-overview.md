## Project Overview

Can AI make game development more accessible for beginners without taking away their creative voice?

**Short Summary:**

This project explores how AI can help beginners start developing their own playable games without any prior advanced coding skills. I am investigating whether AI can act as a supportive tool without diminishing the designer's own voice, choices, and imagination. This project will focus on accessibility, creative ownership, and the tension between assistance and authorship.

**Audience and Context:**

This project is for beginner game creators, design students, and artists/storytellers who have game ideas but may feel blocked by coding and technical barriers. This is being developed in the context of an AI + Design class, where I am researching how AI changes the creative process in game development.

**Possible final directions**

- **Playable prototype:** a small game created with AI support by someone with limited game development experience.

- **Process journal:** a documented record of the creative process where AI helped, where it failed, and where I rejected or revised its ideas.

- **Beginner toolkit:** a guide or workflow showing how non-coders can use AI while keeping creative control.

## Inquiry Brief

**Research Question:**

How can AI help beginners develop playable games while still preserving their creative voice?

**Why is Matters:**

Game development can feel unapproachable to people who have strong ideas for mechanics, storytelling, and/or world-building but lack coding or technical skills. AI tools may close that gap by assisting with coding, art, writing, prototyping, and problem-solving. However, this raises another issue. If AI contributes too much to the process, does the final game still feel like it belongs to the creator?

**What I Need to Learn:**

I need to learn how beginner-friendly AI game development tools actually are, especially for someone without much coding experience. I also need to understand where AI is most useful in the process of game-making, whether it's brainstorming, coding, etc... Most importantly, I need to learn how to recognize when AI supports creative direction vs. when it starts replacing it.

**Possible Final Outcomes:**

1. The AI works well enough to the point where it can help a beginner develop a playable game prototype with minimal complications.

2. The AI either does not help with the game-making process, complicates it further, or strays off and completely takes over the creator's vision.
